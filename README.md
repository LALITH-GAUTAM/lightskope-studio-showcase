[View business website](https://lightskope.com/) · [Client work](https://lightskope.com/portfolio) · [Developer profile](https://github.com/LALITH-GAUTAM)

## My role

Co-founder and web developer. I built and maintain the studio website, collaborating with my co-founder on business and creative work.

## Purpose and implementation

The website presents our web, branding, content, and digital services, showcases client work, and provides a project inquiry process.

**Technology:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Formspree.

The project includes a server-side quote endpoint that validates requests and forwards them to Formspree. Request validation, provider acceptance, and final email delivery are separate workflow stages.

## Project record

This is LightSkope's business website, including its services and portfolio section. This public repository documents the project; application source and operational configuration are maintained privately in `lightskope-creative-studio`.
